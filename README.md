## Problem statement
Employee attrition is costly. This dashboard answers questions such as:
- How many employees have left, and what is the attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles lose the most people?
- Which age groups and tenure bands are most at risk?
- How do gender, marital status and education relate to attrition?

## Key metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total employees | 1,470 |
| Active employees | 1,233 |
| Total attrition | 237 |
| Attrition rate | 16.1% |
| Average age | 36.92 |
| Average salary | 6.50K |

## Key insights
- Research & Development has the highest attrition count (133).
- Sales follows with 92 attrition cases.
- Employees aged 25–34 have the highest attrition count (112).
- Male attrition count is higher than female (150 vs. 87).
- Attrition is highest among employees with 0–2 years at the company (102).
